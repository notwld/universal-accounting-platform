# Research

## Method

- Decision: straight-line by complete calendar month; last month takes remainder so NBV stops at residual.
- Rationale: one formula, matches FIN-09 schedule without tax packs.
- Alternatives: declining-balance, daily proration (deferred).

## GL

- Decision: capitalize Dr cost Cr funding; depreciate/write-down Dr expense Cr accum; dispose reverses cost/accum, Dr proceeds, plug gain/loss.
- Rationale: one posting engine; register NBV = cost net − accum net.
- Alternatives: contra through a clearing account (noisier).

## Impairment

- Decision: write-down is extra accum/expense, not a new method or revaluation surplus.
- Rationale: FIN-09 adjustment without a second workflow.
- Alternatives: revaluation reserve (country-pack later).
